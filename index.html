BWC Asphalt Performance Analysis

Comparison between Control Mix, 2% Biochar, and 5% Biochar
Asphalt Wearing Course – Bitumen Grade 60/70

Project Overview

This study evaluates the effect of biochar modification on asphalt performance. Two biochar contents (2% and 5%) were compared with a control mix using Marshall properties and volumetric analysis.

Engineering Interpretation

  • Biochar significantly increased Marshall stability, especially at 5%.
  • However, 5% biochar caused excessive stiffness, leading to flow values below specification.
  • 2% biochar provided the best balance between strength and flexibility.
  • 5% biochar may still be suitable for heavy-load roads such as truck lanes.
